Support for Humanities Research by Means
of Full-text Search in a Distributed
Environment (project «Humanitariana»)
S. Lyapin, A. Kukovyakin, I. Tolstikova
Justifies the use of advanced full-text search
(including mode of the distributed information
environment) to support humanitarian studies. In this
connection describes the project «Humanitariana» to
create a virtual information resource center to retrieve
contextual knowledge in the Humanities, created on the
basis of the University ITMO (St. Petersburg) with
partners.
