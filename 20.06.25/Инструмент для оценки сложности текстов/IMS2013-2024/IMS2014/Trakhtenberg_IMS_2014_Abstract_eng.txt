Adoption of Healthcare Information
Technologies Through the Eyes of
Physicians: Benefits and Threats
Anna D. Trakhtenberg
This article discusses the results of qualitative
survey conducted in the framework of the project “The
E-Government Implementation as a Process of Social
Adoption of Technology» (grant RHF-Ural number 14-
13-66016a) in Yekaterinburg (Ural Federal District).
The author examines the reasons for organizational
resistance to implementation of the medical information
systems’ (including electronic medical records). It is
shown that the purposes of computerization, as they are
stated by the high level health care management and
implemented by developers come in conflict with the
basic values of the medical corporation (professional
autonomy and monopoly of expertise). As a result, what
proponents see as the benefits, physicians and nurses
estimate as a threat and impossible demands.
In practice, the implementation of medical
information systems leads to a tightening of formal
control and significant employees overload without
appreciable improvement of patients’ treatment.
