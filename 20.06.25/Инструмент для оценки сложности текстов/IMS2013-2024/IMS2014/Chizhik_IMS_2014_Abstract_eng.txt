New Media Formats in Modern Russia
A.V. Chizhik
This article analyzes the existing media formats. In
this study, statistical data showing the presence of
features of traditional media on the Internet and
showing the trends in the development of new media
formats. This article contains charts, which are showing
the ranking of citation and attendance media, author
analyzes the main features of regional and federal
media, and describes the features of the media
interaction with its audience through social networks.
