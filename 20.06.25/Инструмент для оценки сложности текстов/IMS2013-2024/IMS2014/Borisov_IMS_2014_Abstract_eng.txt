Internet and Multimedia Technologies
in the Modern Theater
N.V. Borisov, D.I. Burlov, A.A. Smolin,
D.A. Stolyarov, P.P. Tscherbakov
This article presents the experience of
interdisciplinary work of experts in the field of
information and multimedia technologies from the
ITMO University, together with specialists of the New
Stage of Aleksandrinsky Theatre. As a result of this
collaboration a number of theatrical projects has been
implemented using multimedia and internet technology,
a prototype of multi-purpose hardware and software
system has been developed and outlined a series of
long-term objectives for the implementation of the use
of Internet and multimedia technologies in theatrical
performances.

Government of the Russian Federation, Grant 074-U01
and Saint-Petersburg State University development
