1

The paper presents results of diachronic researches of political terms with the system
Google books Ngram Viewer. We studied fluctuations of frequency the lexical units
in the texts of Russian books in the period from 1920 to 2000. The results show
connection of frequency fluctuation of words with historical events, which allows
various interpretations. This can be of interest both for culturological and for
linguistic studies.
