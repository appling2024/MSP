Russian State Portal as a Technological
Innovation and Problems of Its Adaptation
(on the results of the sociological survey)
A. D. Trakhtenberg
The article discusses the results of testing of the
Russian State Portal www.gosusligi.ru using the model
of innovation adoption (E. Rogers) and method of
cognitive walkthrough. It is concluded that the main
barriers to the e-services adoption is a low compatibility
with previous experience in dealing with the authorities
and the complexity of using (low quality usability) of
the Portal.
