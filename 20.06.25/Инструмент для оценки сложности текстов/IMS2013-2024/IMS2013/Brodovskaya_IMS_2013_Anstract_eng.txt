The political subjectivity of the Russians
in the perception of Internet users: results
of the within the framework
of World Internet Project-2012
E.V. Brodovskaya
The article reflects some results of the research
«World Internet Project in Russia - 2012" realized the
Sholokhov Moscow State University for the Humanities. In the article analyzes the characteristics of the
perception of the Russian users of political subjectivity
in the Internet space, identifies the major trends in the
global network of political functions in modern Russia.
