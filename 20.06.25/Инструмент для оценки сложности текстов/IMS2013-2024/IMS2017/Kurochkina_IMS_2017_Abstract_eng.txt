Evaluation of automatic collocation extractions methods
Y.N.Kurochkina

Saint Petersburg State University

This paper is devoted to the evaluation of the measures of association
used to identify collocations. The goal of the work is to find out how
effective and relevant these measures of the association are, and to show
this with the example of popular measures, namely t-score, MI3, log
likelihood and logDice. The paper deals with the theoretical issues of
collocation extraction, the classification of stable word combinations, and
describes the measures of associations. Further it is described the
experiment on collocation detection from the Araneum Russicum
Russicum Maius corpus with the volume of 1.2 billion tokens in the
NoSketchEngine system, the evaluation of the work of the association
measures is presented.
