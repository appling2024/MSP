The typology of meta-communicative units
in Russian monological spontaneous speech
K. D. Zaides
Saint Petersburg State University
This article is dedicated to the meta-communicative units which occurred in
spontaneous monologues. Meta-communication is the communication about
communication, the special meta-level of the speech, in which the attitude to the
language and its code (in Jacobson’s terms) of native speaker becomes apparent. In
this article it is suggested the typology of meta-communicative units based on their
communicative orientation (to the text or to the listener) and on their main function in
the discourse (discursive, corrective, explicative, evaluative, and searching). It often
appears in such places of the text where are some difficulties in the speech
structuring, for example, in planning monologue. Therefore as the material for the
investigation was used Balanced Annotated Collection of Texts, one of the parts of
Speech Corpus of Russian Communication. All the monologues in corpus present
four communicative scenarios: the reading of a text, the retelling of a text, the
description of a picture and the story. The results of this investigation constitute one
aspect of the special speech grammar, differed from the well-known language
grammar.
