SOME FEATURES OF BRIEF INTERROGATIVE UTTERANCES IN RUSSIAN ORAL
DIOLOGICAL SPEECH
G.M. Sagmanova
Saint-Petersburg state university
Saint-Petersburg
The research is devoted to study of the specifics of the implementation of interrogative utterances in Russian
dialogic speech, which are one of the most frequent elements in spoken discourse and play an important role in
dialogue structuring. As part of the study, a comprehensive analysis was made of 120 utterances, selected from the
array of the recordings of Russian speech corpus "One speaker’s day". The given research considers some features
of realization of question utterances from the point of view of their pragmatic functions, morphological and
structural and syntactic characteristics.
