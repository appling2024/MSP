Specifics of information extractiing for building the ontologies of
poorly formalized domains
G. Artemova1, K. Boyarsky1, N. Gusarova1, N. Dobrenko1, E. Kanevsky2ITMO University, St. PetersburgSt. Petersburg Institute for Economics and Mathematics, RAS
It’s sensible to use e-learning for specialist training in poorly formalized domains.
All over the world domain ontology building is regarded as effective information
support to such approach. Analysis of books on historical shipbuilding defined a
range of difficulties that can occur during text processing for ontology building. They
concern term historical changes, the contradiction of term explanations in given
sources and dictionaries. Besides, the frequency of some terms is quite low.
For information retrieval from texts we used semantic and syntactic parser SemSin
combined with semantic classifier, which helps find not only terms but all words related to this term. Due to semantic classifier, it’s more effective to extract classforming (for ontologies) terms, set class hierarchy, define names and values of properties.
This article includes examples demonstrating the effectiveness of this approach.
