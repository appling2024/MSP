The status of knowledge
in some Text Understanding System
Nina Leontyeva
Scientific Research Computing Centre, Moscow State University
Information vs. linguistic models of natural text analysis are compared by their
tasks, methods and structures. The place & functions of Ontology in some Text
Understanding System are discussed. The term “Ontological Semantics” is specified
as a high level System above all domain ontologies including proper linguistic one.
Units of the proper linguistic Ontology are different from notions of textual semantic
structures. The textual semantic units have to be comparable with units of some
general Ontology, and they may be structured as some initial knowledge base for a
user.
