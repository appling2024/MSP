Collocations regarded as a word
K. Boyarsky 1, E. Kanevsky 2
ITMO University, St. Petersburg,St. Petersburg Institute for Economics and Mathematics, RAS
This article examines stable collocations regarded as a word. They are quite common in the Russian language. The ambiguity of such collocations complicates text
processing. They can be multiword lexical phrases (adverbial, prepositional ones or
comment clauses) or they can be used independently of their components. In certain
cases the same collocations may have several meanings.
Algorithmes of disambiguation for more than a hundred of these collocations were
formulated. They all are based on analysis of context morphology and of semantic
classes of lexical environment. According to the analysis performance assessment, the
accuracy is around 90–95%.
