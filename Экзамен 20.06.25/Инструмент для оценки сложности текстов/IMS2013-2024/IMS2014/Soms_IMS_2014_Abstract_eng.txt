Using Natural Language Processing Tools
in the System of Information Resources
Monitoring by User Preferences
N.L. Soms, A.V. Dobrov, A.E. Dobrova
Modern systems of monitoring of news and social
media resources in most cases do not allow the end user
to customize the immediate procedures of data filtering,
which would be able to give him the opportunity to
keep track of publications on topics created by him, in
view of the indicators of publications' and topics'
coverage of the Internet audience. This article describes
an approach to this problem based on the methods of
natural language processing, developed by the team of
developers of «Geline» LLC, when creating
«SMiRTEO» software system.
