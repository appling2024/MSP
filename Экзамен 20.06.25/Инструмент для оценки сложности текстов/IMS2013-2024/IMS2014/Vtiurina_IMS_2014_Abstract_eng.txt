PR-support of the development of The National
e-Services Portal in Russia
A.S. Vtiurina, O.G. Filatova
This article describes PR and othrts communication
activities of some regions of the Russian Federation,
contributing to the active implementation of programs
for the development of e-government in Russia (on the
example of The National e-Services Portal). This work
includes analysis of the main methods of PR-support for
development EGPU (The National e-Services Portal)
and provides guidance on the establishment of a
universal project PR - support programme for the
development of e-government in Russia.
