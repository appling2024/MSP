Electronic Vote: Methods, Risks and
Problems
O.Y. Peskova, S.V. Fateeva
In article possible risks and problems of systems of
electronic vote, and first of all - remote, are considered.
Risk analysis of remote electronic vote is carried out,
their classification is presented, various groups of risks
are in detail described. The article discusses the existing
methods and systems of implementing electronic
voting: electronic bulletin by Bismarck, Scantegrity, an
electronic voting system in Estonia. The general
classification of similar systems is presented,
experience of their use in the different countries is
described It describes the problems related to the
organization of electronic voting, and the existing
solutions. The ways of development of existing systems
