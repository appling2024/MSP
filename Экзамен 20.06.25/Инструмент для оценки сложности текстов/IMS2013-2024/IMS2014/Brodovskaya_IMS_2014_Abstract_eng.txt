Cybermetrics analysis of public evaluation
of the club formation "School of the great
books" as a method of informal selfeducation of adults in Russia
Brodovskaya E., Dombrovskaya A, Karzubov. D.
The article presents the results of cybermetrics
analysis of the reports related to the estimation of work
of such method of informal education of adults, as
"School of great books". An attempt to use a
cybermetrics analysis as means of measuring of
efficiency of informal educational technology and offer
to recommendation on perfection of her realization is
undertaken.
