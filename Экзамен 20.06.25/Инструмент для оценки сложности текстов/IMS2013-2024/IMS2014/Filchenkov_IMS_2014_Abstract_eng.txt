Comparative analysis of the predict
outcomes of elections and protests tasks
methodological foundations on the basis of
digital traces of users in social media
Filchenkov A.A., Azarov A.A., Abramov M.V.
The problem of forecasting protest activity on
digital traces of social media users is still unresolved, in
addition there is no scientific articles on this topic. This
article uses comparative analysis of this problem and
the problem of forecasting election results, which is
already sufficiently studied. The comparison was
carried out on six selected criteria: representation,
representativeness, openness, theoretical predictability,
the mathematical formulation of the problem and the
availability of data.
