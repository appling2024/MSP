Media Systems of Russia and Brazil at the
Present Stage of Mass Communication
V. Filippova
The paper discusses the main characteristics of
mass communication and modern media systems in
Russia and Brazil. So-called mass media of the first
level – are elite, targeted at ruling classes. All of them
enforce their positions in global space and almost
erase geographical and cultural boundaries.
Audiences of mass media of the second level –
national, they are usually focused on an average and
lowest segment of audience if according to the level
of the income, educational and cultural level.
Informatization and digitalization of mass media lead
to formation of hybrid media systems where the role
of new media, in particular, of social networks and
the Twitter grows more and more
