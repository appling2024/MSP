Automation of Bibliographic Processes
M.A. Belinskaya, I.I. Novitskaya, E.A. Tileva
The report presents the experience of automation of
bibliographic library processes. Considered the most
important directions of the Scientific department of
informatics and automation, such as creation of
electronic catalog, digitization, work on the program
“The scientific heritage of Russian Academy of
Sciences”, development and maintenance of factual
databases on conservation and preservation library
collections.
