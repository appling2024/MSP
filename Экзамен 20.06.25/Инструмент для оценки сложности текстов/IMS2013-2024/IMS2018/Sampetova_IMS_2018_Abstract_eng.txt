IMPLEMENTATION OF THE ALGORITHM FOR AUTOMATIC CORRECTION OF
TYPOS
V.V. Sampetova, V.O. Lukanina, A.D. Moskvina, O.А. Mitrofanova
Saint Petersburg State University
The report presents the experience of implementing the algorithm for automatic correction of typos based on the
Levenstein distance and the n-gram model. In this paper, it was suggested that typos — is incorrect input of words
from any electronic device having a computer keyboard, so the algorithm takes into account the distance between
the keys of the standard keyboard of the Russian language.
