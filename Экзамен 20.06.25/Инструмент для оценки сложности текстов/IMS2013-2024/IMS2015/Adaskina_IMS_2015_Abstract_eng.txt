Semi-Automatic Classification of an Unbalanced Corpus
Yu. V. Adaskina, A. M. Popov, P. V. Rebrova
InfoQubes, St. Petersburg State University
We discuss a semi-automatic classification method for an unbalanced text
collection designed in order to make the time-consuming and tiresome expert work
with a key word based classificatory easier. We propose a text-categorization
bootstrapping algorithm. To evaluate the quality of classification we have manually
annotated a corpus of 620 documents.
Then we conducted a number of experiments. Such parameters as lemmas,
bigrams, syntactic relations and their combinations were considered for effectiveness
evaluation. Moreover, we compared effectiveness indicators for different step size
values, i.e. different numbers of documents, which the expert has to estimate for each
iteration. Our experiments proved that a rather high Recall (0.91) can be achieved by
labelling only 24% of corpus.
