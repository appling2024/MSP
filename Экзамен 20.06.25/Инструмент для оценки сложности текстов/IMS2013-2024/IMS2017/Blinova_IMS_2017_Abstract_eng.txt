«Pejorative plural» in Russian: semantics and сo-occurrence patterns
(web corpus-based study)
O.V. Blinova
Saint Petersburg State University
Russian forms of pejorative plural of the type vsjakie pugachevy-Pl, kirkorovy-Pl
‘various Pugachevas and Kirkorovs’ are derived from the surnames, and express
negative opinion about the referents. This paper aims at corpus-based study of
composition of noun phrases with such forms. The study allows to pick out the stable,
most frequent lexical and other features, supporting the connotation “limited quantity
~ large quantity” and the meaning of non-uniqueness of referents of plural forms in
question.
