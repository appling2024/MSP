REPORT’S TITLE IN ENGLISH
A.A. Ogai
Saint Petersburg State University
Saint Petersburg
This article shall concern dictionaries of scientific works devoted to pre-Cambrian acritarchs and the potential
of three-dimensional visualisation usage for gleaning visual information from them. Multiple variants of said
visualisation shall be viewed, depending on purpose, as well as the data that can be gained from it. Thus we shall see
where said visualisation is useful for frequency dictionaries, both insofar as it concerns the scientific works
researched within this article, as well as for works pertaining to other branches of science.
