Computing semantic similarity of Russian texts by means
of DKPro Similarity tool
A. Kriukova
St. Petersburg State University
This paper looks into a problem of computing semantic similarity of texts in
Russian. In course of experiments we employ an open-source framework DKPro
Similarity, and describe its advantages for this purpose. Our attention is focused on
string metrics of computing text similarity. Experiments are carried out on test
samples including similar extracts from fiction, research, and news texts. For pairs of
texts we use several string-based similarity metrics, implemented in DKPro
Similarity, and pass the computed values as features for machine learning algorithms.
We also present a method of evaluating similarity measures’ relevance for particular
purposes. Results of the research prove that simple string-based metrics contribute to
performance of linear models while trying to identify whether texts belong to the
same group – with average F-measure value 0,88 for eight datasets. In future we also
plan to use semantic text similarity measures which make use of external sources of
knowledge, e.g. Wikipedia, and employ more sophisticated machine learning
algorithms to improve the performance in some difficult cases.
