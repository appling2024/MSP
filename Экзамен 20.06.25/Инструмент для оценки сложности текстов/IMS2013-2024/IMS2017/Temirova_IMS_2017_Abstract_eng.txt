NATIONAL MULTILINGUAL CORPUS NAMED AFTER ABUSUPYAN AKAYEV
D.A. Temirova
Lomonosov Moscow State University
Moscow
The paper deals with the prerequisites for the creation of a National Multilingual Corpus (NMC) named after A.
Akayev. The corpus will act as an array which will include the texts in 4 languages of the Turkic group for the
period from the beginning of the XX century to the nowadays. 7 subsections of NMC will present texts relating to a
certain area and a general section will bring together all the submitted works in the corpus. The generation of such a
corps will provide a wide field for the researches of the comparative analysis, as well as for further translation of
texts and creation of dictionaries on a particular subject
