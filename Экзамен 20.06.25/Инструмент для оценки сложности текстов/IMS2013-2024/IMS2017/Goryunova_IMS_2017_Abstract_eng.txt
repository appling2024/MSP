Using the UML language for modeling and representing ontologies of specialized subject areas
The main principles of ontology classification are investigated in the article. It is concluded that the main
components of the domain ontology are: classes (concepts), relations (properties, attributes), functions, axioms,
instances (individuals). The use of the UML for modeling and representation of the ontological model of the domain
is proposed, which allows creating a thesaurus, formal taxonomies, formal instances, arbitrary logical constraints
(queries).
