Pre-syntactical module of the parser
SemSin
K. K. Boyarsky, E. A. Kanevsky
The principles of work pre-syntactical module of the
semantic-syntactic analyzer SemSin are described. Using of this module allows to significantly reduce the
ambiguity of morphological parsing before the beginning of the syntactical stage. Tokenization procedures
for complex lexical units and phraseological expressions are considered. The paper contains examples of
removing ambiguity of certain words using only the
nearest context.
