Teen Suicide Mirrored by Blogosphere
A.S. Bikkulov
The article describes the problems of teenage suicides in Russia and in the world, and also comments
and discussions for this phenomenon in the Russian
blogosphere. We studied the dynamics of the topic
discussions by the automated social media monitoring
service IQ Buzz, which made it possible to analyze the
fluctuations of the discussion activity during the last
ten years. The article also shows the secondary research data analysis which proves, that reducing the
discussion of this phenomena in mass media we can
reduce teenage suicides.
