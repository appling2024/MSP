A Semantic Model of the “Thesaurus of
Poetics” Forming Part of an InformationAnalytical System
V.N. Boykov, I.A. Pilshchikov
This paper describes the logical-semantic model of
the “Thesaurus of poetics”, which is to become the core
of an information-analytical system of Russian poetry
(under construction). The model corresponds to the fact
that the thesaurus is created as an open network share,
and is intended to facilitate the automatic categorization
of thesaurus and meet the requirements of the analytical
processing of poetic texts
