Cultural Studies in cyberspace: interenetcommunication as a form of social
interaction
Irina Tolstikova
The problem of the socio-cultural specific studying
of the role and place of social networks in the communicative space of the modern society, due to the fact that
networks have emerged as a new level of being, turned
into a kind of global focal point of social ties, based on
new ways of modeling the objective reality and regulating communication processes in social systems for meet
personal and group communication needs.
The relevance of the research work is defined by the
problems of intercultural relations too. On the one hand,
the social processes of information culture create new
opportunities, types and forms of communication between representatives of different cultures, on the other
hand it demonstrates their destructiveness. An important
condition for the effectiveness of communication in
cyberspace is intercultural competence, understood as a
socio-cultural quality of the person required to adapt to
different cultural conditions for successful and effective
cross-cultural communication, collaboration and cooperation.
The actual significance of the research is to study
communication in social networks combined humanitarian, technical, technological expertise in order to obtain
practical conditions necessary for the development and
implementation of creative abilities and personal scenarios, focusing on innovative solutions to the problems
and challenges of modernization of public information
culture.
