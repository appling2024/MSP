Surveys in the Field
of E-Democracy / E-Participation:
Comparative Analysis
R. Bolgov, O. Filatova, A. Chugunov
The article compares five surveys of public opinion
and experts in 2010-2013 covering Russia, the U.S., the
EU, Switzerland and the countries of Central and Eastern Europe. The authors have developed a list of criteria
for comparing the attitude of citizens and experts to edemocracy. The comparison was performed on the most
important sectors as well as issues, threats and challenges that respondents see in e-democracy and eparticipation. The similarities and differences in attitude
of citizens and experts from these countries to edemocracy and e-participation were highlighted.
