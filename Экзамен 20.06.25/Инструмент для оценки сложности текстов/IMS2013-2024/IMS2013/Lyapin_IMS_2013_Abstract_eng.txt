Digital full-text library for support of
analytical & research working
S. Lyapin, A. Kukovyakin
The report discusses the use of the services of multifunctional digital full-text library, created on the basis
of the information system of the T-Libra 6.6.x («Constanta» Ltd., Arkhangelsk, Russia), to support analytical
and research activities. Examples of such use are given:
for support of the management activity in the sphere of
culture (analytics of reports, thematic analysis of information materials); activities of the Museum (the information-analytical support of exhibitions and expositions); humanitarian researches in the sphere of philosophy (explication of semantic contexts of Russian
philosophy)
