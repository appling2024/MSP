Anti-Piracy Law:
The response analysis in social networks
D. Juk, S. Petuhov
The results of the monitoring Comments on social
networks topics related to «anti-piracy laws nom»,
which came into force on 1 August 2013. As the tool
was used for the analysis of social media monitoring
service IQBuzz. The article provides data analysis of
the reaction online community, including private users,
the introduction of a new law on the protection of intellectual property for the period 15 July to 30 September
2013.
