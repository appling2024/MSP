Extracting Term Candidates for Multilingual Glossary of Terms
S. Pivovarova
Saint Petersburg State University
The paper is aimed at describing a procedure for extracting term candidates from a
parallel corpus for a multilingual (English-French-Russian) glossary of terms. The
procedure is based on the extract-then-align algorithm which consists of two main
steps: extracting term candidates (using morphosyntactic patterns and association
measures) and matching translation equivalents. The algorithm is then applied to the
parallel English-French-Russian corpus of international legal documents. The paper
provides the analysis and evaluation of extracted term candidates
