Interpretation of Semantic Relations in
the texts of the Russian LiveJournal
Segment based on LDA Topic Model
S.N. Koltsov, O.Ju. Koltsova,
O.A. Mitrofanova, A.S.Shimorina
The paper deals with the experimental results of
topic modeling performed on the Russian LiveJournal
Corpus with the help of TopicMiner tool based on
LDA (Latent Dirichlet Allocation). Our data allowed
to form the set of topics related to the posts of LJ users, and to describe the main types of semantic relations within topics. The results of our research can be
explained from the point of view of the theory of lexical functions, frame semantics and construction
grammar. Semantic analysis of topics reveals dynamic processes in language perception of Russian LJ
users.
