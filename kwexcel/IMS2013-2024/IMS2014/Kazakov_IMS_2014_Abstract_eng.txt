Phenomena of Social Network Services:
Issues of Terminology and the Approaches
to Platform Organization
V.G. Kazakov, I.N. Karpenko
Under the scope are the problems of social network
services and ways to overcome them. The incoherence
and inconsistency of terminology using to describe the
social networks and social networks services is
discussed. A number of fundamental problems of social
network services are identified and described. Shown
that these problems seem to be insurmountable in the
traditional architecture of building such services.
Discusses possible ways of organizing the systems that
implement similar functionality A new method of
organized the social network services, based on the
multiagent systems are proposed.
