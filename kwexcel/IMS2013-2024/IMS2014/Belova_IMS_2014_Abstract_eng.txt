Advertising on the Internet as a Tool for
Promoting Social Project «E-Citizen of the
Nizhny Novgorod Region»
L. Belova
This article is devoted to the ability of using the
Internet to promote the social projects in the sphere of
information society realized by the Government of the
Nizhny Novgorod Region on the example of the
"Electronic Citizen of the Nizhny Novgorod Region" .
In the framework of this scientific work there are
presented proposals for a campaign to promote the
project using the online tools and carried out the
analysis of the target audience of the project
