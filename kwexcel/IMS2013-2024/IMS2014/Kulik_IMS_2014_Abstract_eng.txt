Governance 2.0 to Challenge Systemic
Crisis of Government
A.N. Kulik
The paper presents the author's view on the
evolution of government paradigm at the end of the
twentieth – the beginning of the twenty first
centuries driven by two main factors – the sociocultural transformation of the postindustrial society
and the Internet-revolution. The development of
Web 2.0, the growth of interactive capacity of IT’s,
and the rapid expansion of access to broadband
mobile Internet contributed to government
efficiency enhancement trough implementing the
conception of Collaborative governance that
assumed wide civil society involvement in decision
making process.
