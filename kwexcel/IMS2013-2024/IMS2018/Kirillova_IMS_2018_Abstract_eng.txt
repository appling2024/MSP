Testing and assessment of the NLTK4RUSSIAN hybrid morphological analyzer
A.K. Kirillova1, A.G. Melnik1, A.D. Pletneva1, E.V. Enikeeva1, O.A.Mitrofanova1
1

Saint-Petersburg State University

This paper describes a series of experiments aimed at testing the hybrid morphological
analyzer NLTK4RUSSIAN on the datasets of the Dialogue Evaluation 2017. We provide a
detailed explanation of the principles and main operation modes of the system. Experiments on
training the system were based on RNC and OpenCorpora subcorpora. Certain problems dealing
with the data format were solved in course of research: we performed conversion of Universal
Dependencies markup format to PyMorphy2 format. The analyzer was tested on social network
subcorpus, news subcorpus and fiction subcorpus. Test results for the NLTK4RUSSIAN
morphological analyzer correspond to the gold standard of morphological annotation for the
Russian language.
