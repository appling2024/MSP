Formation of information culture as a
factor of implementation of the concept of
the electronic government in Russia
D.E. Prokudin
Development of information society in Russia, implementation of the concept of the electronic government accompany different problems. In article the analysis of the problems connected with formation of individual information culture which is a basis of readiness
of citizens, representatives of the business and authorities to use of information and communication technologies when rendering and receiving the state and municipal services is given. Ways for the solution of these
problems being in the plane of an education system are
offered. As one of them development and creation of
the educational Internet portal "Information Society"
which will carry out an integration role in all educational and methodical, directory and consulting activities for maintenance of use of information systems and
formation of information culture within development of
information society in Russia generally and implementation of the concept of the electronic government in
particular is offered. The purposes, tasks and results of
development of the educational Internet portal "Information Society" locate.
