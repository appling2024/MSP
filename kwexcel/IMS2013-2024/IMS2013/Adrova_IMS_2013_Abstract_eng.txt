Research of hashing algorithms which are
used for authentication of Web-applications
L.S. Adrova, P.N. Polezhaev
This article is devoted to the research of hashing algorithms which are used for authentication of Webapplications. Execution time analysis of algorithms has
shown that the best option is SCRYPT algorithm with
parameters (27, 28) and (28, 27). The optimal number
of external rounds was chosen for GOST and SHA512
algorithms
