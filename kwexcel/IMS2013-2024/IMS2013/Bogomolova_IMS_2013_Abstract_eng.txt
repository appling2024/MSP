UIS RUSSIA: Experiment on Public
Administration Domain Ontology as a Tool
for Socio-Economic Statistical Data and
Analytical Publications Integration
A. V. Bogomolova, N. F. Dyshkant, A. Yu. Krylov, O.
V.Petukhova, T. N.Yudina

The paper reports on the experiments undertaken by
University Information System RUSSIA team to exploit
the public administration domain ontology, created
under the project to provide for domain knowledge
organization and for a value-added search. The ontology
described has proved its appropriateness for statistical
data and analytical publications search and contentbased integration. Further work on ontology evaluation
is needed and is planned to be done to improve the
results.
Ontology is a perspective field for research and
applications, dynamically developed during last
decades. Public administration is under increasing social
attention throughout the world due to its effective
performance being crucial for any country development
and competitiveness in the future. Public administration
has become a knowledge consuming field.
To implement public administration ontology-based
data and knowledge products integration is a challenge
for investigators and a social demand.
