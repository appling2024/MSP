Semantic trasformation of political vocabulary
Andrey Ts.Masevich 1, Victor P. Zakharov2,
1
St.-Petersburg State Institute of Culture,
2
St.-Petersburg State University
The paper presents results of diachronic research of the frequency behavior of three items of Russian political
vocabulary. As a tool, we used the Google Books Ngram Viewer to create a set of charts of the frequency behavior
of three selected items. We found that within a given chronological period connotations of the items have changed.
The correlations with the historical process were supposed.
