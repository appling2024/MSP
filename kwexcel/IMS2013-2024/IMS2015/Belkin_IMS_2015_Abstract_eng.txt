Semantic network structures for knowledge space of
subject domain «Historical demography»
V.Y. Belkin, K.I. Kostenko, B.E. Levitskyi, V.N. Rakachev
Kuban State University
Results of the development and application of weakly formalized knowledge
domains models creation technology are presented in systematic way. This
technology is founded on the system of the unified structural and functional invariants
of the applied intellectual systems formal models. These invariants are applied for
developing the uniform and connected semantic representation of the content of
knowledge domain "A Historical Demography". The formalism of hierarchical
semantic networks is the cornerstone of methodology of creation of subject domains
knowledge spaces.
Results of development and application of weakly formalized knowledge domains
models creation technology are presented in systematic way. Such technology is
based on intuitively complete system of the unified structural and functional
invariants of the universal subject domain knowledge space model. Invariants are
realized for applied intelligent systems of subject domain "A Historical
Demography". These invariants provide possibility of considered knowledge space
unified and connected semantic structure, constructed by elementary and complex
fragments of knowledge representations. Special semantic networks structures are
proposed for description of demography processes and semantic structure of scientific
papers in demography. The formalism of hierarchical semantic networks is applied as
foundation of methodology of subject domains knowledge spaces models creation.
System of knowledge space processing operations is based on association approach to
classification of intelligent system problems and problems solving algorithms. All
these operations are realized by special program tools. They simulate different aspects
of decomposition and structuring processes of weakly formalized subject domain’s
intelligent resources.
